# FAQ Accordion Widget

A **responsive FAQ accordion widget** built using **HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**. This widget allows users to easily browse through frequently asked questions with smooth collapsible animations and a professional, clean design.

---

## **Features**

- **Collapsible Answers** – Click on a question to reveal or hide the answer.  
- **Smooth CSS Transitions** – Animations for opening and closing answers.  
- **Arrow Indicators** – Arrows rotate to indicate open/close state.  
- **Mobile Responsive** – Works seamlessly on all screen sizes.  
- **Clean & Professional Design** – Minimalistic styling suitable for websites or portfolios.  
- **Optional Single-Open Behavior** – Only one FAQ item can be open at a time (configurable in JS).

---

## **Technologies Used**

- **HTML5** – Structure of the FAQ section.  
- **CSS3** – Styling, layout, and transitions.  
- **JavaScript (ES6)** – Toggle open/close states for FAQ items.
